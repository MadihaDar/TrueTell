CASE FILE 01 — WHY THIS MATTERS

"Check your sources" was built for a world where lying took effort.

It doesn't hold up against content that's generated in seconds, sounds completely real, and is engineered to make you feel something before you think.

"She didn't fall for it because she wasn't careful. She fell for it because the advice she grew up with was built for a different kind of lie."

We started this project after a friend's mother lost PKR 50,000 to a scammer using a cloned voice recording — a voice that sounded exactly like someone she trusted. Going through past media-literacy programmes, we noticed something else: almost all of them are built for classrooms. The young people most exposed — first-time voters, youth outside formal education, community-group members with no media training — are the ones no one is really reaching.

TARGET AUDIENCE — PRIMARY

First-time voters, 18–25

Young people in Pakistan about to vote for the first time, or active in a youth organisation, sports club, or community group — scrolling past AI-generated political clips, health scares, and "breaking news" every day, with no framework for slowing down.

TARGET AUDIENCE — SECONDARY

Youth workers & community leaders

The people these young people already trust. Hand them something they can run themselves, and this stops being our project and becomes theirs.

CASE FILE 02 — THE METHOD

SEE-IT: five questions for anything in your feed

Before you believe it, before you share it — run whatever you're looking at through these five checks. They work whether it's a headline, a photo, or a voice note.

S

SOURCE

Who posted this first, and are they actually who they claim to be?

E

EVIDENCE

Does this show up anywhere else, from someone independent?

E

EMOTION-CHECK

Is this trying to make me feel something before I've had time to think?

I

INTENT

Who benefits if I believe this and share it?

T

TRACE

Can I trace this back to where it actually started, or does the trail just stop?

CASE FILE 03 — THE KIT

Two formats, so nobody's left out because of a phone or a signal

Most digital-only media-literacy tools quietly leave out whoever doesn't have reliable data or a smartphone. TrueTell exists in two forms on purpose.

FORMAT A — DIGITAL

TrueTell Digital

A 10-minute quiz in your browser, no install. You're shown a real mix — an image, a headline, a voice clip, a quote — some genuine, some AI-generated. Run it through SEE-IT, make the call, then see exactly what gave it away.

  • 8 real, documented cases — no new fakes generated
  • Score at the end, plus the tell for every round
  • Works on any phone browser, nothing to download

FORMAT B — OFFLINE

Immunity Circle

A 60-minute session any youth-group leader can run with printed scenario cards. No internet, no media-literacy background, no need for us to be in the room.

  • Facilitator guide with full session script and timing
  • Printable scenario cards for group discussion
  • Built to be picked up and reused without us

CASE FILE 04 — WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT

Trained against the actual thing that's fooling people

Most media-literacy tools teach caution in the abstract — "be skeptical," "check your sources." TrueTell flips that: the training material is real, already-documented AI-generated content, used transparently to build resistance to itself.
